Worth understanding
Generative AI — it responds
You give it a prompt. It gives you content — text, images, code. It reacts. Every response starts fresh.
ChatGPT answers questions, writes text
Claude / Gemini drafts, summarises, explains
Agentic AI — it acts
You give it a goal. It plans steps, uses tools, takes action, and keeps going until done.
Claude Code writes, tests & deploys end-to-end
AutoGPT multi-step task automation
Simple example: GenAI writes the email you asked for. Agentic AI researches the recipient, writes it, sends it, follows up if no reply — without you asking again.
